Product thesis

Sofi should work for your wealth,
not for the checkout button.

A shopping agent can optimize price and rewards. A wealth-aligned agent should also know when a higher reward creates a worse outcome for the user.

Never make me poorer to make my cashback bigger.

The product principle

  • Understand the purchase intent before searching.
  • Make the user's budget and autonomy rules explicit.
  • Optimize effective cost before reward percentage.
  • Keep purchase authority deterministic and user-controlled.
  • Measure wealth created from existing spend, not GMV alone.

1. Start with intent, not filters

Sofi asks one question that reduces the most uncertainty.

YouI need running shoes under ₹6,000. I care about cushioning, don’t care about brand, and I don’t want to spend more just for rewards.
SofiGot it. One trade-off matters most here: if you had to choose, would you prefer maximum cushioning or lighter weight?

2. Turn intent into a mandate

The model can reason inside these boundaries. It cannot silently rewrite them.
